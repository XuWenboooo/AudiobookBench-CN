# Top-Conference Phase Handoff Boundary

The archived Week1–5 material motivates a new research phase but cannot close
its confirmatory question.

OLD_MODEL_SELECTION_RESULTS_CAN_DEFINE_CONFIRMATORY_WINNER = NO

## Question boundary

Old phase motivation: “What preliminary evidence motivated the new research
question?”

New confirmatory question: “Whether Detection and Localization exhibit
systematic robustness decoupling, and whether a bounded adversary can
selectively exploit it.”

The new question requires a separate preregistered population, estimands,
adversary budget, model-selection rule, and outcome-blind confirmatory gate.
Week1–5 outcomes may motivate those choices but may not select the final
confirmatory winner after observing results.

## Phase separation

No immediate top-conference experiment is authorized. Permitted preparation is
limited to a dedicated top-conference branch/worktree, empty namespace
directories, a preregistration skeleton, and a novelty ledger skeleton.
Training, new generation, adaptive attack execution, use of a held-out
confirmatory population, and outcome-driven model selection are prohibited
until `TOPCONF_RESEARCH_PREREGISTRATION_V1` is frozen and separately
authorized.
