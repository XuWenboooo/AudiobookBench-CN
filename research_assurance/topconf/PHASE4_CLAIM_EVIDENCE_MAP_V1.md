## Evidence exclusions

Week1–5 pilot observations, Phase3T Level-1 diagnostic values, synthetic
simulation outputs, checkpoint-load smoke results, and failure patterns may
support provenance, engineering compatibility, or design rationale. They may
not support a confirmatory effect, a baseline ranking, a threshold, a sample
size update, a mechanism choice, a novelty upgrade, or a causal claim.

Every future result must link to the frozen protocol, dataset/freshness
manifest, model/checkpoint identity, output namespace, terminal ledger,
primary metric implementation, uncertainty procedure, and the corresponding
claim row. Any post-reveal protocol change is exploratory and receives a new
version.
